> "Some doors, once opened, can never be closed." - Silco

## About

Arcane is the first animated series from the **Riot Games** of the hit game League of Legends and **Fortiche Production**, It follows the story of Two Adopted Sisters that Exist in the Game itself, Vi and 'Powder', whose relationship got torn apart by war, politics, tragedies between the cities of **Piltover** and **Zaun**.

---

## Main Characters

|Character|Description|
|---------|-----------|
|Vi|Older sister, strong and protective|
|Jinx (Powder)|Younger sister, unstable and unpredictable|
|Jayce|Hextech inventor from Piltover|
|Viktor|Scientist who wants to evolve humanity|
|Silco|Crime lord of the undercity Zaun|
